Insurance payment guide

Allstate insurance payments

Multiple ways to pay, with discounts that may be available.

Last reviewed: September 18, 2026 · Written by a licensed insurance professional

Quick answer

Allstate commonly accepts payments online, through its mobile app, by phone, by mail, or through an agent office. Availability, fees, discounts, and posting times depend on your policy and location.

Payment options

Online

Use Allstate’s official account or payment experience.

Mobile app

Check the current Allstate app listing and account requirements.

Phone

Call the verified billing number listed on your bill or official site.

Mail or agent

Allow delivery time and confirm the correct address before mailing.

Discount questions to ask

Full pay

Ask whether paying the premium in full changes the price.

AutoPay

Confirm eligibility, timing, and payment-source requirements.

Paperless
