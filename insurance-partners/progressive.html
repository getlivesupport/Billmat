Progressive insurance payments

Simple ways to pay your premium and understand your options.

Last reviewed: September 18, 2026 · Written by a licensed insurance professional

Quick answer

Progressive commonly offers online account payment, Quick Pay, mobile app, phone, and mail options. Discounts, fees, posting times, and grace periods vary by state, policy, and eligibility. Confirm current terms directly with Progressive.

Pay without logging in

Progressive’s official Quick Pay page may allow a payment without signing in. Use the official link below and confirm the web address before entering information.

Potential savings

Pay-in-full, AutoPay, paperless, and other discounts may be available depending on your state and policy. Any example savings must be confirmed in your quote and billing account; there is no universal 15% guarantee.

Frequently asked questions

Can I pay Progressive without logging in?

Progressive provides an official Quick Pay path. Availability and required details can change, so use the official website.

What is Progressive’s pay-in-full discount?

It varies by state, policy, and eligibility. Ask Progressive for the exact amount shown on your quote or account.

How long is Progressive’s grace period?

There is no single universal answer. Check your policy documents and state-specific terms.

Does Progressive charge fees for monthly payments?

Installment and payment fees may vary. Review your billing statement or ask Progressive before selecting a plan.
