Contact BillMat LLC

Reach BillMat for content questions, updates, and corrections

Use this page if you want to report a broken link, ask for a wording clarification, or let us know a billing guide appears outdated. BillMat cannot access your policy, take payments, reinstate coverage, or handle claims.

Last reviewed: September 18, 2026Editorial review: BillMat updates this page whenever correction and contact instructions change.

How to contact us

BillMat handles website questions, not insurance account actions

The available contact method for site corrections is the public repository issue tracker at github.com/getlivesupport/Billmat/issues. When reporting a content issue, include the page URL, the detail you believe is wrong or unclear, and any official source that supports a correction. This helps us review your note more efficiently.

If your concern involves a payment due today, policy cancellation, coverage question, roadside event, or claim, contact the insurer directly instead of waiting for BillMat. We do not have account access and cannot intervene with an insurer on your behalf.

What BillMat can help with

Content questions and factual corrections

  • Broken or outdated official insurer links
  • Headings or wording that need clarification
  • Visible typos or formatting problems
  • Requests to add a verification reminder where a page feels unclear

What BillMat cannot do

Policy access, payment handling, and claims support

  • We cannot accept or process insurance payments
  • We cannot look up policy balances, due dates, or reinstatement status
  • We cannot file, update, or track a claim
  • We cannot promise insurer-specific discounts, hours, fees, or grace periods

Response expectations

What to expect after sending a correction request

BillMat reviews reasonable correction requests as time allows. Some requests lead to immediate wording or link updates; others require a broader review because a single billing detail can affect multiple pages. We do not guarantee personalized responses, but we do use reader feedback to improve the site.
