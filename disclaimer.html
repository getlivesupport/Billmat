No payment processing

All payment actions must happen on the insurer's official website or support channel

BillMat does not provide a checkout page, payment form, account login, or policy-servicing tool. If you click a payment link from BillMat, you are being directed to the official insurer website. Always verify the web address before entering any personal or payment information.

Information may change

Use the site as a guide, not as a final source of record

Billing links, service hours, mailing addresses, fees, grace periods, discounts, and phone prompts can change over time or vary by state and policy. BillMat works to keep pages useful and current, but you should verify any live billing detail with the official insurer before acting.

No policy access

BillMat cannot view your policy or confirm your account status

Because BillMat does not have insurer account access, it cannot tell you whether a payment posted, whether your policy is in force, whether a discount applies, or what your due date is today. Those answers must come from the insurer or your policy documents.
