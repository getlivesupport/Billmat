Privacy policy

How BillMat handles site data and third-party links

This privacy policy explains what information BillMat LLC may collect when you browse the site, what it does not collect, and how cookies, analytics, and external links are handled. Effective date: September 18, 2026.

Last reviewed: September 18, 2026Editorial review: BillMat updates this page when analytics, cookies, or contact practices change.

What we collect

BillMat is designed to collect as little information as possible

BillMat is a static website. When you browse pages, the site itself is not designed to ask for a payment card, bank account number, or insurance login. BillMat does not provide a billing form, payment portal, or policy lookup tool.

Like most websites, basic server and hosting logs may record technical details such as IP address, browser type, device information, referring page, and timestamps. Those logs are used for security, troubleshooting, and routine hosting operations.

What we do not collect

No payment data, policy credentials, or claims information

BillMat does not collect or store insurance payment credentials, card numbers, bank details, policy login usernames, claim information, or personal billing balances. If you choose to visit an insurer website from BillMat, that transaction takes place on the insurer's own systems and is governed by the insurer's privacy practices.

Cookies and analytics

Cookies may be used for basic functionality or general website measurement

BillMat may use limited cookies or similar technologies for basic site performance, security, and anonymous traffic measurement. If analytics are used, they are intended to help understand which pages are useful, which links break, or how the site can be improved. BillMat does not use the site to build a payment profile or insurance underwriting profile about visitors.

If you do not want cookies stored on your device, you can adjust your browser settings. Disabling some browser storage features may affect normal site behavior, but the site is intentionally lightweight and should remain broadly usable.

Third-party links

Official insurer links lead to websites outside BillMat's control

BillMat links to insurer websites so visitors can verify payment options and take action with the official source. Once you leave BillMat, the privacy practices of the destination website apply. BillMat is not responsible for the content, security, or privacy handling of third-party websites, including official insurer websites.

Children's privacy

The site is not intended for children seeking financial or insurance services

BillMat is written for a general audience seeking insurance education. It is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a BillMat contact channel, use the contact page so the issue can be reviewed.

Data requests

Questions or requests about your information

If you contact BillMat about privacy or content, the site may retain the information you send in order to respond, investigate a correction, or keep a record of the request. If you want to ask about data related to a message you sent BillMat, use the contact instructions on the contact page. If your concern involves information held by an insurer, contact that insurer directly because BillMat does not control insurer account systems.
